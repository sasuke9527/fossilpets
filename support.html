常见问题

宠物多久会饿一次？
各项数值按真实时间缓慢下降，每天照看一两次即可。离线期间最多按 12 小时结算，长时间不玩不会回来发现宠物"惨不忍睹"。
怎么让宠物进化？
主界面的进化进度面板会列出全部条件（成长天数、羁绊值、规律睡眠）。达成后点"进化！"播放演出。
完全体 A 和 B 有什么区别？
照顾失误少且作息规律 → A 分支；否则 → B 分支。两者都是正式形态，没有优劣之分。
免费版和完整版的区别？
免费版可饲养 4 只、只能随机挖蛋；一次性购买「解锁完整版」后：饲养栏位无上限、可指定进化线挖蛋（同一条线也能再挖）、全图鉴进化树立即揭晓。无订阅、无广告、无消耗品。
换了新手机，怎么恢复购买？
用同一 Apple ID 登录，进入 App 的「设置 → 恢复购买」即可。也支持家庭共享。
神话瑞兽类怎么解锁？
其余每个大类至少养出一只完全体后自动解锁，挖掘页会显示进度。
进度会同步到 iCloud 吗？
当前版本存档保存在设备本地。iCloud 同步在后续版本的计划中。

Fossil Pets · Support

Questions or feedback: acct9527@gmail.com (usually answered within 2 business days)

How often do pets need care?
Meters drop slowly in real time — checking in once or twice a day is enough. Offline time is capped at 12 hours of decay.
How do I evolve a pet?
The evolution panel on the main screen lists every condition (days grown, bond, sleep rhythm). Tap "Evolve!" once they're met.
Ultimate A vs B?
Few care mistakes and a regular sleep rhythm lead to branch A; otherwise B. Neither is stronger — they're two collectible forms.
Free vs Full version?
Free: 4 pet slots, random digs only. One-time "Full Unlock": unlimited slots, targeted/duplicate digs, and all evolution trees revealed. No subscriptions, ads, or consumables.
New phone — how do I restore?
Sign in with the same Apple ID and use Settings → Restore Purchases. Family Sharing is supported.
How do I unlock the Mythical class?
Raise at least one line in every other class to an Ultimate form. The Dig tab shows your progress.
